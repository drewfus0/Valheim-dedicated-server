⏱️ Valheim Timers
Spawn, Growth & Production Trackers
Presets:
🫐 Wild Berries & Foraging (5 Hours)

Raspberry, Blueberry, and Cloudberry bushes take 300 minutes (5 real-world hours) to respawn. Wild bushes never permanently deplete.

💡 Survival Tip: Never destroy the bushes with tools! World time must pass in active loaded sectors for respawn checks to advance.
🌾 Cultivation & Crops (4000s - 5000s / ~1h 15m)

Domesticated plants (Carrots, Turnips, Onions, Barley, Flax, Jotun Puffs, Magecap) take 4000 to 5000 seconds (average 4500s / 75 minutes, or ~2.1 in-game days) to mature.

💡 Cultivation Rule: Plants must have cultivated soil, open sky above (no roofs), and at least 0.5m clearance. Crowded crops turn brown and wither.
🔥 Surtling Core Spawners (5 Minutes)

Swamp fire geysers spawn 3 Surtlings every 5 minutes (300 seconds) as long as fewer than 6 Surtlings are in the area.

💡 Core Farm Automation: Use a pickaxe to lower the ground around the fire vent until water covers the soil. Newly spawned Surtlings instantly die in water, dropping Surtling Cores and Coal automatically!
🍯 Beehives & Honey (20m / 80m Full)

Each happy beehive generates 1 Honey every 20 minutes (1200 seconds), holding a maximum capacity of 4 Honey (full after 80 minutes).

🍺 Fermenter & Mead (40 Minutes)

Converting a mead base into 6 usable potions takes 2400 seconds (40 real-world minutes). Must be sheltered under a roof.

⛏️ Smelters & Charcoal Kilns (14s / item)

Processes 1 ore / wood every 14 seconds. 10 items take 2m 20s; a full 20/25 load takes approximately 5 minutes 50 seconds.
